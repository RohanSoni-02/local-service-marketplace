# Thikana — feature list

## Discovery
- Eight service categories; shops ranked by rating within a category, by distance on home
- List and map views (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap)
- Shop profile: verification tier, rating, jobs done, distance, hours, gallery, reviews, call and message
- Stock search across nearby shops' inventory, in-stock and nearest first

## Service visits
- Request form: issue type, description, as-soon-as-possible or a scheduled slot, visit address, optional budget
- Broadcast to up to four shops in the trade; offers arrive with price and ETA
- Accept, decline, or counter once; the shop accepts the counter or holds its price
- "Compare offers" off: the first shop to accept gets the job at its rate
- Requests lapse after 10 minutes without an acceptance
- Live status timeline: sent → confirmed → on the way → completed
- Cancel until the technician sets out
- Rate and review after completion; the shop's rating updates

## Tool rental
- Per-day price and refundable deposit, 1–14 days
- Reservation with a 4-digit pickup code; a reserved or rented tool shows as unavailable to others
- Pickup, due date, return; pay at the counter

## Parts on hold
- Hold one unit for two hours with a pickup code; stock is decremented and restored on cancel or lapse

## Society group booking
- Search and join a society with a flat number, or register one (creator becomes admin)
- Admins post group requests; residents add or remove their flat
- Providers quote per flat with a minimum flat count; admins confirm a quote once the minimum is met

## Account
- Phone + password sign-up and sign-in, 30-day sessions, sign-in throttling
- Edit name and address; activity history for visits, rentals and holds
- Chat threads with shops, opened automatically when a shop accepts

## Not built yet
- Merchant app (shop responses are simulated — see README)
- SMS OTP verification, in-app payments, photo attachments, Hindi localisation
